## 2. What the research proved (confirmed claims only)

Every item below survived 3-vote adversarial verification. Citations in the research artifact.

1. **Primary GBP category is the #1 local-pack ranking factor** — a strict filter for which queries you can rank for at all. Held #1 for six years. Wrong category is the harshest penalty.
2. **Map-pack ranking = Relevance + Prominence + Conversion, together** — no single hack. Behavioral/conversion signals (click-to-call, frictionless contact) are real 2026 ranking inputs and drive ~5x conversion.
3. **Review VELOCITY beats raw count** for a low-review profile. Steady **1-5 reviews/week, forever.** Recency drives "near me." Flat-lining causes ranking drops. Respond to reviews 2-3x/week.
4. **Buying/incentivizing reviews is actively harmful** — high-velocity fake reviews get deleted within ~6 days; April 2026 Gemini-powered enforcement. Safe organic ceiling ≈ 1-5/week.
5. **Penalty-safe authority = real, geo-relevant local links**: chambers/associations, partner/supplier pages, local sponsorships, local causes, local PR, high-quality curated niche directories. **Relevance + geography beat volume. 10 quality local links beat 200 spam directory links.** Bought links = short boost then penalty (real-time Penguin + SpamBrain).
6. **Blog 0-clicks = AI-Overview casualty** — informational-query CTR fell ~65% (1.76% → 0.61%). Scoped to *informational* queries; commercial pages far less affected (AIOs trigger on only ~8% commercial / ~5% transactional queries).
7. **Earning an AI-Overview citation = +120% clicks** vs uncited (informational), but still -38% vs no-AIO. Mitigation, not full recovery.
8. **Position 6-20 pages → improve titles + answer customer questions + chunk Q&A.** BUT this site's commercial pages rank 21-91, *outside* striking distance — they need authority first.

### Myths the research KILLED — do NOT waste time on these

- ❌ GBP posts quantity, Q&A volume, keyword-stuffed GBP description, geo-tagged photos → **no ranking impact** (Google strips photo EXIF). Use posts/photos for *conversion*, not ranking.
- ❌ "Secondary categories dilute your primary" → refuted. Using relevant secondary categories is fine.
- ❌ "+35%/+91% AIO citation lift", "32% GBP / 20% review weight split", "10-review threshold", "no link source >30-40%" → all failed verification. Don't anchor on them.
- ❌ BrightLocal's Turkey citation list → **all international platforms, zero TR-native directories.** Useless for local citations; we source TR directories independently (see §6 open items).

## 3. Strategy in one sentence

**Stop making pages. Concentrate everything on the two missing levers — Local/GBP (fast) and real local Authority (slow) — recover the clicks already being earned, and watch it on a weekly scoreboard.**

A key reallocation the data forces: the **district pages** (organic, thin terms, ranked 40-91) are a weak bet. The right way to capture "[district] web tasarım" intent is **GBP service-areas + local pack**, which ranks *above* organic and moves *faster*. So district targeting pivots from thin organic pages → GBP local pack.

## 4. The four pillars

### Pillar 1 — LOCAL / GBP (highest near-term leverage, fastest results)

- **Lock the primary category** to the exact closest match (e.g. "Web tasarımcısı / Web designer"). Add relevant secondary categories (Website designer, Internet marketing service, Graphic designer) — not harmful, adds query eligibility.
- **Review-velocity engine** (the single most actionable move): steady **1-5 real reviews/week, forever.** Build an ask-list of past clients; ask via WhatsApp/SMS/email after every project and back-fill old clients. **Never buy.** Respond to every review 2-3x/week.
- **Complete every relevance field**: full services list with descriptions; **service areas = the Istanbul districts** (this replaces the district-page bet); natural business description (don't keyword-stuff — doesn't help ranking).
- **Conversion**: click-to-call, WhatsApp link, frictionless contact directly on the profile (real ranking input + 5x conversion).
- **NAP consistency** identical everywhere (name/address/phone).
- **Weekly GBP post** — for freshness/conversion, explicitly NOT a ranking lever; keep it cheap (15 min).

### Pillar 2 — AUTHORITY (moves commercial pages from 21-91 → striking distance)

Earn REAL, geo-relevant Turkish links — quality and locality over volume:
- İstanbul Ticaret Odası (İTO) / relevant chamber & business-association listings
- Partner/supplier/tool pages (hosting, collaborators, any vendor that lists clients)
- Local event / cause / meetup sponsorship (link + local relevance)
- Local PR angle (e.g. free site audit or pro-bono site for a local nonprofit → coverage)
- Vetted curated directories — **including web-design-relevant ones that also send leads: Clutch, Sortlist, DesignRush** (international but topically relevant)
- TR-native directories (must be researched + vetted — see §6)
- **Velocity**: slow, real, a few quality links/month. Never buy. Expect **3-6 months** of steady links before commercial pages climb into striking distance.

### Pillar 3 — CTR & ON-PAGE (recover clicks already earned)

- **#1 fix — the cost blog post** (`/blog/web-sitesi-maliyeti-istanbul-2026/`, 1,878 impr, pos 5, 0 clicks): rewrite title + meta to be more click-worthy than the AI Overview answer (specificity, "gerçek fiyat tablosu 2026", a hook AIO can't replicate); add a hard internal link/CTA funnelling readers into the commercial `/web-sitesi-fiyatlari/` page. Single highest-ROI on-page action.
- Light title optimization on the 1-2 other buyer-adjacent blog posts (avukat). Do NOT over-invest in informational posts — they're AIO casualties and not buyers.
- Verify schema present (FAQ / LocalBusiness / Service / Article) — mostly done already.
- **Do NOT on-page-tweak the position 21-91 commercial pages yet** — won't move without authority. Optimize their titles only once authority lifts them to 6-20.

### Pillar 4 — MEASUREMENT (the scoreboard — makes it a game)

- Weekly automated GSC snapshot (`~/mcp-google-ads/gsc_report.py`, token fixed 2026-06-09) tracking: clicks, **real-term impressions (junk "web development" filtered out)**, and positions of ~10 target pages over time.
- GBP Insights weekly: profile views, calls, direction requests, review count.
- Monthly review → double down on whatever moved.
